SMALL BY DESIGN

Fits your website.
Keeps its own space.

One loader adds a floating launcher and an isolated chat panel. Your existing theme can control it, while AI credentials and processing stay on SS07’s backend.

One page session

Closing keeps the chat. Reloading or Start Over clears it.

No visitor accounts

The assistant needs no visitor sign-up or owner dashboard.

Privacy considered

No transcripts saved by default. Avoid sensitive information.

Honest limits

Usage caps, bounded context, timeouts, and helpful Retry controls.
